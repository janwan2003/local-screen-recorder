Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This policy covers the Diskreel website, the web recorder at /app/ and the Diskreel browser extension.

Summary

Diskreel collects nothing. It has no server, no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your recordings never leave your computer unless you send them somewhere yourself.

Recordings

When you record, your browser gives Diskreel the screen, window or tab you choose, and the camera and microphone if you turn them on. Diskreel encodes the video on your computer and writes it to your browser's private storage for this site or extension (the Origin Private File System). When you press Save, a copy is written to a file you choose. Recordings stay in browser storage until you delete them in Diskreel or clear the site's data.

Settings

Your choices (camera on or off, which microphone, bubble size and corner) are kept in your browser's local storage so they are remembered next time. They are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

The extension requests no browser permissions. Camera, microphone and screen access are asked for by your browser each time it is needed, and you can withdraw them in the browser's site settings.

Hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) when you load the page; see GitHub's privacy statement. Diskreel itself adds no cookies or analytics.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/local-screen-recorder/issues.
